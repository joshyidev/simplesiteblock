# Privacy Policy

SimpleSiteBlock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What stays on your device

Settings, custom rules, downloaded block-list contents, and the optional options-page password hash are stored locally in your browser using the browser's built-in extension storage. SimpleSiteBlock does not send this data to the developer or any analytics service.

## Network requests

The only outbound network requests SimpleSiteBlock makes are to fetch the block-list URLs you choose to add on the options page, including scheduled automatic updates if you enable them. The extension does not add your browsing history, blocked sites, custom rules, or settings to these requests. As with any web request, the list host may receive normal request metadata such as your IP address, user agent, and the requested list URL.

## No analytics or tracking

SimpleSiteBlock contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or any other form of usage tracking.

## Permissions

The extension uses the following browser permissions solely to provide its core blocking functionality:

- **webNavigation** — to detect and block navigations to sites on your lists.
- **tabs** — to redirect blocked navigations to the blocked-page screen, close blocked tabs when configured, and check incognito tab state.
- **storage / unlimitedStorage** — to save your lists, rules, and settings locally.
- **alarms** — to schedule automatic list updates at the interval you configure.
- **http:// and https://** host access — to fetch block lists from the URLs you provide and to intercept navigations.
